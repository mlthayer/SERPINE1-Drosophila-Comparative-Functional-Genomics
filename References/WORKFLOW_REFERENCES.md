# Workflow-specific references

These references concern code, public expression datasets or sequence annotations
used in the workflow. General biological background references are excluded.
Numbers below preserve the author's source-list identifiers, not manuscript order.
Publication citations alone do not identify the exact downloaded dataset release;
see DATA_AVAILABILITY.md and source manifests for release/accession information.
Software-specific bibliography from installed package citation records is in
software_citations.bib. The Biostrings package record there supplies its proper
software attribution rather than the malformed author field in the supplied list.
