## Primary Spn42Da structural study

Ellisdon AM, Zhang Q, Henstridge MA, Johnson TK, Warr CG, Law RH, et al.
High resolution structure of cleaved Serpin 42 Da from Drosophila melanogaster.
BMC Structural Biology. 2014;14:14.
https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6807-14-14
(Author bibliography reference 40.)

The uploaded primary article was inspected directly. It documents the A/B
shared RCL, the 392-residue construct, P1 R342/P1prime Ala and structural
evidence consistent with that cleavage in crystal form 1. The +32 mapping
to the 424-residue supplied A1Z6R1 sequence is explained in
[the primary-source audit](../Sequence/RCL/Citation_Audit/Spn42DaB_PRIMARY_SOURCE.md).
Crystal form 2 has a different observed cleavage position; do not imply both
forms establish the same reactive bond. This is not direct evidence for a
signal-peptide cleavage boundary at full-length residue 32.
